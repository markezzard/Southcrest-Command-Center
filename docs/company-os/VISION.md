# Vision

## Companies

- **Meridian Land** — business development, land searches, contract negotiation, due diligence, entitlements / approvals / permitting.
- **Southcrest Management** — site development and construction once projects are permitted (and also smaller remodel / maintenance / positioning jobs).

Ownership context: 50% owner of both companies.

## Destination

Build an agent workforce that helps run company operations on clean shared data, eventually giving each employee role-native agents.

Examples of the end state:

- A **superintendent agent** watches schedule + email, proposes daily work, requests specific field photos, and checks evidence against plans.
- A **PM agent** tracks commitments, delays, RFIs/follow-ups, and escalations.
- A **BD agent** supports lead generation for both companies and relationship continuity with schools/districts.
- An **executive agent** briefs ownership on stalled deals, permit risk, schedule slips, and attention needed.

## Operating philosophy

1. **Foundation before autonomy** — Project identity, Drive discipline, email resolution, Procore sync, and a clean database come before unsupervised action.
2. **Human-gated stages** — Agents classify, file, propose, and draft first. Outbound email, legal/commercial actions, and live system mutations require approval until trust is earned.
3. **Systems Analyst → builders** — Capture intent and sources, define charters, then spawn focused builder/data agents one slice at a time.
4. **Shared data spine, personal agent UI** — Company systems of record first; employee agents are configured views/workers on top of that spine.
5. **Repo docs are long-term memory** — Important decisions live here, not only in chat.

## Dual BD funnel (high level)

Business Development serves both companies:

- **Meridian path:** school contacts interested in expansion → land / site / entitlements lifecycle.
- **Southcrest path:** remodel, maintenance, and smaller jobs that deepen school relationships and position the companies for future expansion work.

One school relationship may generate Southcrest work now and Meridian expansion later.

## Success looks like

- Every serious project/site has stable identity across Meridian App, Procore, Drive, and email matching.
- Emails and attachments are reviewed, classified, and filed with provenance.
- Procore schedules remain the long lifecycle operational timeline (often 3–5 years, sometimes 10+ with phases).
- Employees receive trustworthy short lists of what matters, not noisy autonomous churn.
